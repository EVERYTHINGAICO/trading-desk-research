# Doctoral-Grade Research Method

The proposed research contribution is a protocol for reducing false strategy promotion, not a claim that a named strategy is profitable.

## Evidence Progression

| Class | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| E0 | Idea or retrospective observation |
| E1 | Exploratory replay or engineering feasibility |
| E2 | Preregistered historical confirmation |
| E3 | Frozen prospective Shadow evidence |
| E4 | Reconciled small-notional Demo observation |
| E5 | Independently reproduced result |

Promotion requires preregistration, causal timestamps, complete trial accounting, dependence-aware inference, multiple-testing correction, realistic costs, locked evaluation, prospective evidence and independent reproduction.

The normative materials live in the repository's [`docs/research`](https://github.com/EVERYTHINGAICO/trading-desk-research/tree/main/docs/research) directory.
