# Everything AI Co Trading Desk Research System

An experimental system for studying algorithmic trading strategies without confusing software behavior, simulated execution, or Demo account movement with proven edge.

!!! warning "Research boundary"
    The included strategies are experimental. They have not been validated with sufficient rigor and have no proven trading edge. This project is not financial advice and does not authorize live-capital deployment.

## What Is New Here?

The research contribution is a provenance-first protocol for measuring and controlling false strategy promotion under temporal dependence, multiple research attempts, realistic execution costs, and prospective evidence collection.

The engineering contribution connects:

1. causally available market observations;
2. immutable strategy and configuration identities;
3. Shadow and Binance Futures Demo execution;
4. native protection and fallback monitoring;
5. position, fill, funding, and commission attribution;
6. reconciliation, incident evidence, and research gates.
